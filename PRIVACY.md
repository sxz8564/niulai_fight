# Privacy

Niulai Fight collects nothing and sends nothing. What it remembers — your
settings and your own ranking board — it keeps on your machine, and it has no
way to do anything else with it.

There is no analytics, no telemetry, no account, no sign-in and no network code
of any kind in the extension. The whole game — its code, its 3D characters and
its artwork — is packaged inside the download and runs on your own machine. It
works with the wifi switched off, which is the simplest demonstration that
nothing is leaving.

The extension declares no permissions and no host access. Clicking its toolbar
button opens a page inside the extension itself. It cannot see, read or act on
any other page you have open, because it was never granted the ability to.

## What is kept, and where

The game remembers a few things between visits, in your browser's own storage
for this extension, on this machine:

- your settings — music on or off, difficulty, which backdrop, infinite mode,
  and whether you have been shown the tutorial;
- the ranking board — the scores of runs you chose to save, and the name you
  typed for them.

The name is optional, it is yours to make up, and saving a run is something you
have to press a button to do. All of it stays on this machine: there is no
network code in the extension to send it anywhere, and no server on the other
end if there were. Removing the extension removes it, and clearing the
browsing data Chrome keeps for the extension clears it too.

Nothing is shared between machines, which also means the board is your own —
your runs, not the world's, and the game says so on the screen that shows it.
